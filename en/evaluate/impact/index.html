    Method & limits

    A discussion aid, not a scientific measure.

    The short form retains two items per dimension from the development long form. This reduction supports initial screening but also reduces coverage of each dimension.

    01Nine dimensions shown separately

    Six Gollac factors and three exploratory AI-specific mechanisms.

    02No diagnosis

    The result replaces neither work analysis nor clinical or legal expertise.

    03No individual ranking

    The questionnaire concerns a project and work situation, never an individual worker.

    04No decision threshold

    Continuing or stopping a project requires documented discussion, not an automated score.
