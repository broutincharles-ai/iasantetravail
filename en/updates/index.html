01 / Editorial method

Three simple rules.

The aim is not to accumulate links, but to show where a claim comes from and how far it can reasonably be used.

01 · SELECTION

Primary sources first.

Official texts, research, prevention bodies and provider documentation. Comparators add context but never support a conclusion alone.

02 · QUALIFICATION

Information status is explicit.

Current rule, future application, observed result, consensus, uncertainty and prevention recommendation are distinguished in the wording.

03 · CORRECTION

Material changes are traceable.

A factual error is corrected once verified. Changes in method, source or interpretation appear in the log below.

02 / Review frequency

Review according to obsolescence risk.

A date is not a permanent guarantee; it marks the last time the content was reviewed.

MONTHLY + EVENT

Law and governance.

Monthly review and review after a major regulatory or case-law development.

MONTHLY

Model landscape.

Review of families, methods and links, brought forward if a change makes the page misleading.

EACH VERSION

Questionnaires.

Review after any change to an item, calculation, report, export or methodological status.

QUARTERLY

Educational content.

Scheduled review supplemented by reported corrections and important new publications.
