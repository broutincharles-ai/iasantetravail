Editorial method · Version 1.0

Making the evidence, the limits and the corrections visible.

AI & Occupational Health favours primary sources, distinguishes established results, emerging signals and hypotheses, then dates every sensitive piece of content. A review statement is displayed only when an identified person has genuinely reviewed the scope concerned. Reported errors are verified, corrected and recorded without erasing the useful history.

01 / What the site publishes

The status of a piece of content must be legible before it is interpreted.

SYNTHESIS

State of knowledge.

Several sources are brought together, their methods and limits are specified, without turning an association into causation.

EXPLORATORY TOOL

An aid to discussion.

A questionnaire or grid structures observation. It replaces neither a diagnosis, nor a regulatory assessment, nor a validated instrument.

FORESIGHT NOTE

An explicit scenario.

A hypothesis is presented as such, with its conditions, its counter-arguments and what would make it testable.

PUBLICATION

Work published elsewhere.

The record points to the journal, the institution or the DOI that constitutes the bibliographic source of truth.

02 / Evidence hierarchy

Four levels, never a truth score.

The level describes the nature of the available support. It never removes the need to read the method, the population, the context and the limits.

LevelSupportEditorial use
APrimary source, official text or systematic review

Establish a rule, a definition, a method or a result within its exact scope.

BSingle empirical study

Document a signal while retaining the limits of sample, measurement and generalisation.

CProfessional consensus or documented field report

Describe a practice or a framework for action without presenting it as causal evidence.

DHypothesis, scenario or analogy

Open a research or prevention question, with an explicit statement of uncertainty.

Order of preference for sources

Official text or original study → public body or learned society → literature review → documented professional source. Media and commercial communications can flag a case, but do not on their own form the basis of a conclusion.

03 / Review and freshness

Frequency depends on the risk of obsolescence.

An ‘updated’ date is not enough: living pages specify what has been checked and, where possible, the next review.

CONTINUOUS

Law and governance

Monitoring whenever a text, timetable or doctrine changes; formal review at least quarterly.

6 MONTHS

Scientific evidence

Re-reading of sources, new work, consistency of evidence levels and maintenance of links.

EVERY VERSION

Tools and methods

Version, purpose, items, rules of use, limits and change log are updated together.

12 MONTHS

Stable pages

Check of intent, clarity, internal links, metadata and author identity.

External review

The name, qualification and scope of review are shown on the page only when an external review has actually taken place. In the absence of that statement, no external validation should be inferred.

04 / Corrections

Correcting without silently rewriting history.

  1. 01Receive

    The report identifies the page, the passage and, where possible, a verifiable source.

  2. 02Verify

    The content is compared with the source of truth and with the exact scope of the claim.

  3. 03Correct

    The change is dated; a substantive correction is summarised in the page change log.

  4. 04Propagate

    The translations, tables, diagrams and structured data concerned are checked in turn.

05 / Independence and responsibility

An independent resource is not an institutional source.

The project is run by Dr Charles Broutin. His professional affiliations shed light on his experience; they do not mean that a cited organisation endorses the site as a whole.

Responsibility

The author is answerable for the selection of sources, the wording, the limits and the corrections, within the exact scope stated on each page.

Conflicts and funding

Any funding, partnership, contribution or interest liable to influence a piece of content must be declared on the page concerned. A link to a supplier does not constitute a recommendation.

Scope

The site provides general information on prevention and method. It constitutes neither individual medical advice nor legal advice tailored to a particular situation.
