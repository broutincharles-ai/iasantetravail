Artificial intelligence and occupational health.

Artificial intelligence is transforming work, its organisation and the practices of professionals who support employers and workers.

AI & Occupational Health brings together the knowledge needed to understand these transformations, prevent their effects on health and use AI judiciously in professional practice.

An editorial initiative by Dr Charles Broutin, occupational physician and AI lead for the French Society of Occupational Health · Updated 11 September 2026

Essential

An information, training and prevention resource.

In occupational health, artificial intelligence is both a professional tool and an organisational transformation. Its effects on workload, autonomy, skills, teams and health depend on actual use, objectives and deployment conditions—not only on the performance of the model.

AI & Occupational Health offers an open pathway to understand systems, identify risks, assess uses, organise prevention and govern deployments.

FOR YOU

See how this issue affects you.

Introducing AI changes more than the tool you use. It can alter your workload, autonomy, how your work is monitored and the responsibility you carry when something goes wrong. Safety therefore also depends on work-design choices, the time available to check outputs, the ability to challenge a system output, training and reporting routes.
