What AI changes at work.
And how to prevent the risks.

This independent initiative connects occupational health and AI safety, with the ambition of becoming a reference in France and internationally.

By transforming work, AI affects our health, our autonomy and the balance of economic and social power. Occupational health provides essential knowledge and means of action to understand these changes, anticipate their risks and protect workers. It must also help guide the design and deployment of safer AI.

Dr Charles Broutin, occupational physician · AI lead for the French Society of Occupational Health (SFST)
