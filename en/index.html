01 — THE PROBLEM

How does AI affect
occupational health?

Artificial intelligence can change tasks, deadlines, room for judgement, responsibilities and skills, with possible effects on occupational health.

These effects depend not only on technical performance, but on how a system is deployed, controlled and discussed. Prevention therefore means examining real work before deployment and after use begins: cognitive load, autonomy, surveillance, work quality, collective work and health. This concerns employers, worker representatives, public authorities and occupational safety and health professionals together.

02 — THE ARGUMENT

A model never acts alone.

The health effects of an AI system do not come from the model alone. They emerge from the way its capabilities are integrated into the organisation of work.

05 — ABOUT

An occupational physician’s perspective on AI deployment.

AI & Occupational Health is an independent editorial initiative led by Dr Charles Broutin, an occupational physician and AI lead for the French Society of Occupational Health. The project connects the evaluation of AI systems with the analysis of real work: what tools enable, what they shift within an organisation and what must be verified to protect health.
