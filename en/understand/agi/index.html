AGI: what are we talking about?

Several laboratories say they are trying to build artificial general intelligence. This page summarises the competing definitions, what the measurements show in November 2026 and what that implies for work.

By Dr Charles Broutin, occupational physician · Updated on 13 November 2026

Read the guide chapter →Guide contents →

The essentials

A stated goal, a disputed notion.

What can be said without betting on a date.

Direct answer

Artificial general intelligence, or AGI, is a goal stated by several laboratories. There is neither a shared definition nor a test that would establish its arrival.

Announcements are not measurements: measured, dated indicators are more informative than statements from players engaged in a race for funding.

Guide, chapter 09

01 / Definitions

Three ways of defining it.

Each sheds light on what is at stake for work.

By work

Outperforming humans at most jobs.

OpenAI’s charter (2018) aims at “highly autonomous systems” that outperform humans at most economically valuable work. The definition is explicitly economic: it is measured by the substitution of human work.

OpenAI Charter, 2018 ↗
By levels

A progression rather than a threshold.

Google DeepMind crosses the level of performance, from “emerging” to “superhuman”, with the breadth of tasks covered. The degree of autonomy left to the system is treated separately: it is a deployment choice, not only a capability.

Morris et al., 2023 ↗
By cognition

Comparing with the abilities of an educated adult.

A group of researchers measures models on ten broad abilities of human cognition. GPT-4 scored 27% and GPT-5 57%, with a very uneven profile: extensive knowledge, almost no long-term memory.

Hendrycks, Bengio et al., 2025 ↗

03 / Debate

Two readings of the same curves.

The arguments on each side, without settling the matter.

Arguments for a rapid arrival

Why some expect an acceleration.

Measured capabilities have progressed steadily for several years. Tests designed to resist models fall quickly: ARC-AGI-3 went from under 1% to 81% in less than eight months. The compute and investment devoted to training are still rising sharply.

Arguments for caution

Why others remain sceptical.

Continual learning and lasting memory remain very limited. Scores depend on the protocol. Reliability on long tasks is progressing more slowly than the demonstrations. Passing tests does not guarantee holding a job in a real environment.

04 / At work

For occupational health, the issue is not the date.

What is worth tracking from now on.

The issue is not to bet on a date. It is to track, task by task, what is becoming automatable, at what pace and under what control, and to anticipate the effects on workload, autonomy, skills and employment before they impose themselves.

OpenAI’s definition is measured by the substitution of human work: that is the scenario examined in the pathway AI and jobs.

Key answers

Three frequent questions.

Short answers, drawn from the guide.

Does AGI already exist?

Nothing allows that to be established: there is neither a shared definition nor an arrival test. OpenAI’s president said in September 2026 that he personally considered it reached. On the cognitive grid of Hendrycks, Bengio and colleagues, the last model assessed, GPT-5, scored 57%.

When will AGI arrive?

Nobody knows. Several laboratory leaders publicly mention a horizon of a few years; the International AI Safety Report considers both a plateau and a sharp acceleration plausible by 2030.

What is the difference between AGI and superintelligence?

In Google DeepMind’s grid, superintelligence is the last level of a general AI: doing better than all humans on many tasks. In 2023, the authors classed it as not reached.
