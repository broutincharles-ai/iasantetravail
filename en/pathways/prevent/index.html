03 / The pathway

Detect early. Prevent over time.

Occupational health connects the analysis of real work, health monitoring and collective prevention. These resources support action with workers, employers and their representatives.

Tools in the guidesWorks council checklist AI and CSE rulings Pilot file Assessment before / after

Practical foundations

  1. 01
    Guide

    Recommendations for using AI well at work

    Six good-practice flowcharts for using AI at work: occupational physicians and nurses, employers, works council members, prevention specialists, employees and researchers.

  2. 02
    Tool

    Assess and monitor an AI use through real work.

    Document a workplace AI use at T0, review observed effects at T1 and monitor nine separate work-related axes over time.

  3. 03
    Guide

    The French CSE and AI at work: assess a project before issuing its opinion

    How should a French Social and Economic Committee (CSE), the works council, assess workplace AI? Consultation duties, psychosocial risks and a practical 10-question checklist.

  4. 04
    Guide

    AI at work: what obligations for the employer?

    What are the obligations before deploying AI at work? AI Act, GDPR, CSE, DUERP, DPIA and governance: a practical, sourced and up-to-date guide.

  5. 05
    Resource

    Prevention fact sheets

    Seven downloadable sheets on AI and psychosocial risks: a cover and six Gollac dimensions, with warning signs, prevention measures and French legal references.

In real work

  1. 01
    Reading

    Is high-performing AI enough to improve work?

    AI effects vary across tasks and workers. Uniform productivity expectations can turn deployment into a prevention issue.
