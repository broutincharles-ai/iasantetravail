03 / The pathway

Prevent the risks of AI at work.

From understanding the system to monitoring its effects: four steps to act with workers and their representatives.

01 Understand AI02 Assess a project03 Act and govern04 Monitor and adjust

01

Understand AI

Identify what a system can do, its limits, and the conditions needed for effective human oversight.

  • Systems, tasks and skills
  • Errors and checks
  • What human oversight requires

02

Assess a project

Start with real work: who is affected, what will change, and what needs to be documented before a trial?

  • Assess the project before and after deployment
  • Questions for employee representatives
  • Compare technical performance with real work

03

Act and govern

Set rules collectively, preserve the means to check and challenge decisions, and adapt work organisation.

  • Recommendations for taking action
  • Prevention fact sheets
  • Responsibilities and governance
  • Define the rules of use

04

Monitor and adjust

Monitor health, skills and autonomy. Use feedback to change the system, adapt its use or stop it.

  • Organise follow-up over time
  • Decide whether to continue, adapt or suspend
