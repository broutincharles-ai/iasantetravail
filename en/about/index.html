02 / Publications

Research connecting language models, work quality and prevention.

Two publications addressing different questions: the psychosocial effects of introducing LLMs and their use as a second reader of medical wording.

2025Psychosocial risks

Large language models and emerging psychosocial issues at work.

The article proposes examining the introduction of LLMs through the six factors in the Gollac report: intensity, emotional demands, autonomy, social relationships, value conflicts and insecurity.

Archives des Maladies Professionnelles et de l’Environnement, 86, 102877.

Open the DOI ↗
2026Quality of recommendations

Artificial intelligence to improve the precision of occupational physician recommendations.

The study evaluates a language model as a second reader for identifying several categories of defects, without replacing work-post analysis or the occupational physician’s decision.

Références en Santé au Travail, TF 335, no. 186, June 2026.

Read the article on the INRS website ↗
The common thread

In both cases, the aim is not to delegate judgement to the model. It is to understand what its introduction adds to human work, what it enables and what it requires people to verify.
