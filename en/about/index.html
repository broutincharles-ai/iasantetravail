Dr Charles Broutin

Occupational health and AI safety.

I am an occupational physician, AI lead for the French Society of Occupational Health and head of the Training & Methods Unit at SPSTN.

My work connects occupational risk prevention with AI safety: protecting health, preserving human agency and understanding how AI changes work.

Contact me on LinkedIn ↗My publications →

What I work on.

Prevent AI-related risks.

Assess how AI use affects workload, autonomy and skills, with the people concerned.

Preserve human agency.

Study losses in the ability to understand, decide and challenge, and their potential value as early warning signs of broader human disempowerment.

Examine safety work.

Understand the conditions in which teams develop, evaluate and oversee AI: available resources, verification workload and the ability to raise concerns.

This approach informs my research, teaching and AI evaluation projects. This website is an independent initiative to make it accessible to occupational health professionals and the AI safety community.
