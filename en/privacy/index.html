Privacy policy

Technical data, local search, external links and visitor rights.

No account or form

This version of the website provides no user account, contact form or proprietary newsletter subscription. The website does not automatically collect email addresses.

Local search

Internal search runs in your browser. Search terms are not sent to the editor or to a third-party search service.

Language preference

When you arrive on the homepage, the website automatically displays the French or English version according to the languages declared by your browser. Your manual FR or EN choice is stored as a simple code in the browser’s local storage. This preference is not sent to the editor or used for advertising. You can remove it in your browser settings.

Audience measurement with Google Analytics

The website loads Google Analytics 4 through the Google tag associated with measurement ID G-RKEJVY4XVC. This service can measure visitor numbers, sessions, pages viewed, traffic sources and approximate information about location, browser and device type. Its purpose is to understand overall use of the website and improve its content.

In its standard configuration, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including _ga, and send Google technical data about the visit. Do not enter personal, medical or confidential information into the website’s interactive functions. Data collected by Google Analytics ↗ · Google Privacy Policy ↗

Cookies and analytics retention

The lifetime and retention of identifiers and statistics depend on the Google Analytics configuration and browser settings. Google states that the GA4 cookies _ga and _ga_<container-id> have a default lifetime of two years, subject to browser limitations and the settings applied to the service. You can delete or block these cookies in your browser settings.

Local questionnaire data

The assessment tools run locally and may retain up to 50 results in your browser to show an aggregate local history. No identifying data is requested and no collective transmission is active while no collection endpoint is configured. You can erase these records by clearing this website’s local data in your browser settings.

Technical hosting

The website may be hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data required to secure and deliver the service, including connection logs, under its own privacy policy.

External links and services

Opening a publication, LinkedIn, Substack or another external website is governed by that service’s privacy policy. Before any visitor action, the browser currently loads Google Fonts and the Google Analytics tag described above.

Contact and data minimisation

Contact is voluntary through LinkedIn. Do not send health data, confidential company information or sensitive personal data.

Your rights

Where information is sent directly to the editor, you may request access, correction or deletion. You may also contact the French data protection authority, CNIL.

Update

Last revised: 30 August 2026. This page will be updated before any change to forms, collection, retention periods or service providers.
