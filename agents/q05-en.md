# What should I do when someone challenges my decision?

ID: Q05 | Record: R05 | Version: 1.1.0 | Date: 2026-09-13
Reference: https://www.iasantetravail.com/agents/#Q05
Publisher: IA & Santé au Travail — https://www.iasantetravail.com/

## Answer

Treat the challenge as information to examine. Explain the basis of the decision, identify the contested facts or criteria, and check them against the relevant records. Correct an established factual error.

For a material unresolved disagreement, make it possible to reach a responsible human with the authority to change the outcome. Depending on the consequences and applicable process, consider deferring the disputed action while it is reviewed. Do not treat a request for review as evidence of poor performance.

## Example — hypothetical

If a worker says your lateness record is wrong, compare the original records and agreed start time before using that record in an evaluation.

## Before acting, check
- Data provenance, missing values, and known limits of the score.
- Decision criteria and what the worker can inspect or correct.
- Who can reverse the decision and how quickly review occurs.

## Options to compare
- Treat the score as one input and examine relevant context.
- Provide an accessible human review with power to change the outcome.

## What to follow up
- Time to review and proportion of decisions corrected.
- Recurring errors and uneven consequences across comparable roles.

## What makes the situation more protective
The person can understand the basis, correct facts, and obtain a substantive review.

## Ask for human review when
Relevant facts are disputed or the decision has material employment consequences.
